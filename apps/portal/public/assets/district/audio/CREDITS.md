# District audio credits

All runtime audio is local. The source pages below were read on 27 November 2026; they explicitly identify CC0. The pack archive also includes the original Kenney license. SHA-256 hashes for the downloaded source files and every generated clip are in [manifest.json](manifest.json).

| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Short Whisper, ID 584650 | LisadiKaprio | [Recording](https://freesound.org/people/LisadiKaprio/sounds/584650/), CC0 | Public HQ preview downloaded; trimmed, quiet mix, original low-frequency texture. Nonverbal human whisper. |
| Cough (2), ID 151212 | OwlStorm / Ashe Kirk | [Recording](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/151212/), CC0 | Public HQ preview downloaded; trimmed, gain/speed adjusted, layered as a short fictional vignette. |
| Cough Female, ID 151213 | OwlStorm / Ashe Kirk | [Recording](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/151213/), CC0 | Public HQ preview downloaded; trimmed, gain/speed adjusted, layered with the other cough. |
| Original procedural layers | Generated for Iva Games by the checked-in Python generator | [CC0-1.0](LICENSE.txt) | Seeded noise, impulses, resonances and envelopes; no recording or voice performance is claimed. |

The epidemic cue is an edited collage of two individual recordings, not a recording of an actual crowd. Weather, insect, dog, machine and water textures are deliberately stylised procedural placeholders. Their quality and recognisability still need human listening. No sound has been taken from Colonist or the accepted chess banks.
