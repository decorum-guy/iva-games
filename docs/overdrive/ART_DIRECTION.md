# OVERDRIVE — visual and sound direction

Owner brief: every Chess 2 game must have an independent identity, models, effects and interface. OVERDRIVE must feel designed around energy and positional abilities. Preserve REMORSE's marble, selected audio and existing interface.

## Chosen direction

A precision competition instrument: a machined aluminium chassis, blue ceramic playing surface, porcelain and cobalt pieces with exposed copper power elements. The memorable object is the arena itself, with two five-segment capacitors integrated into its player rails. It is not a racing game skin; each visible control represents a chess decision.

Palette: cloud `#e5edf1`, aluminium `#bccbd3`, porcelain `#f6f7f3`, cobalt `#245782`, midnight blue `#12334d`, signal orange `#f26330`. Orange denotes charge expenditure and special actions, not every interactive element. White/blue ownership remains readable during effects.

Typography: Russo One for the OVERDRIVE wordmark and short display headings; Golos Text for Russian/English playing controls and explanations. Self-hosted fonts, their source licences and exact hashes accompany the files. No decorative fake telemetry, floating SaaS cards, neon gradient washes, emoji pieces or marketing hero ahead of the playable board.

Desktop: the board dominates the middle; player/energy rails sit above and below. A compact side workbench changes from game setup to the selected piece's actual legal actions. Energy controls sit beside the action they enable. Mobile: the same board and player rails, with the workbench below, bounded by the viewport; rules/history are deliberate overlays with internal scrolling.

## Models

Six original solid 3D models are generated by `scripts/generate-overdrive-models.mjs` and served locally as GLB. A shared stepped mounting foot creates family resemblance while different crowns make piece identity visible at small sizes: pawn ball, aerodynamic horse profile, split bishop cap, rook's open turret, radial queen crown, king's cross. Copper collars are independently named meshes for animation and material emphasis. These are new geometry, not the REMORSE set with replacement colours.

The board uses individual inset ceramic tiles, corner fasteners, a solid structural rim and cooling slots. Its lighting uses broad neutral sources; highlights describe curvature without obscuring a cell. 2D uses original vector silhouettes with the same ownership palette.

## Motion

Both renderers use the pure event-derived choreography in `src/overdrive/ui/motion.ts`. `sceneTiming(events, reduced)` supplies millisecond contact, landing, promotion, income, result and expiry times. `piecePoseAt(motion, pieceId, elapsedMs, reduced)` supplies the visible type, position, lift, opacity and scale. Audio converts the same event times to seconds. A renderer does not decide legality or modify the committed game state.

| Action | Committed movement shown |
| --- | --- |
| Sweep | The pawn stays still; the forced target slides to the front cell. The trail follows the target's actual path, despite the ability actor having identical source/destination cells. |
| Raid | The knight's path visits the victim's cell at the capture instant, then continues to the empty landing cell. The victim fades at that midway contact. |
| Battery | The bishop rises in its source cell, traverses at a foot height of 1.35 cells, then lands. This clears the tallest current screen model, the 1.1-cell king. |
| Ram | The rook approaches first. The target stays still until contact, then both pieces complete the displacement. A forced knight slides without an ordinary knight jump. |
| Rotation | The queen and ally follow opposing lateral arcs, retaining clearance between their mounting feet. Both finish in the committed exchanged cells. |
| Cover | The king vacates first; the ally then fills the former king cell. The replacement does not move before the king has cleared it. |

Captured pieces remain in the presentation union of before/after IDs until their contact time, then fade. A promotion uses the pawn model during travel and replaces it only after arrival, through a short fade between the old and new models. The same handling applies to a forced enemy pawn promotion. Ordinary castling animates both committed move events on one clock. Ability trails are sampled from the same trajectories; all transient effects reach zero before the scene releases input.

Overdrive activation expands the actual named copper collar mesh and warms its material; it is not solely a ring drawn above the board. Its remaining own turns appear explicitly. Check accents the king after the action resolves; mate adds a restrained king tilt and the result panel. Reduced motion uses source/destination fades without translation arcs, jump height or scaling pulses. Promotion retains a short arrival fade before changing the model. Mute cancels audio scheduling. A new move cannot run before the prior move's reading interval.

The motion regressions use real core transitions for all six abilities, capture, castling, ordinary/forced promotion, reduced motion and effect expiry. Their timed coordinates validate sequencing and geometry; they do not establish visual quality, FPS or browser rendering. The 2D projection and audio transport have separate tests against the same shared times.

## Audio

The shipped bank contains 18 original pre-rendered DSP samples made specifically for this arena. No Kenney sample is included: the official packs were considered, but no usable source archive was obtained. `AUDIO.md` records exact provenance, composition, durations, measured peaks and the limits of verification. Perceptual listening and mix acceptance remain distinct from decoding and numerical analysis.

Six abilities have separate cues, in addition to move, capture, charge, full cell, activation, expiry, promotion, check and result. There is no continuous ambience. The capture sample starts at the shared contact time; promotion starts at the model-change sequence; actual resource income and check/result follow resolution. Event layers use a bounded bus; operating-system volume is untouched.

## Acceptance evidence

Geometry dimensions, asset checksums, decoded audio measurements, rule-driven component tests and visual browser observations are separate evidence. Do not claim a render, listening session, FPS or physical phone check unless performed. Current browser restrictions are tracked in `STATUS.md`.
