# COLOSSUS — art direction

Owner requirement, 26 November 2026: rarity must be visible from the entire card background; boss cards are a small memorable set. This is the original implementation direction, not user visual acceptance.

## The reliquary arena

A heavy carved arena of cold stone, with ivory expedition pieces and three monumental adversaries. Bronze inlay marks selection, commands and protective wards. The cards are illuminated engraved relics with individual illustrations. Their rules live on a quieter lower field of the same rarity colour.

Palette: deep slate #182126, stone #35434a, ivory #e1d8bd, aged brass #b29863, frost #8db7b8, wound red #b96054. Card materials: common slate, rare lapis blue, epic amethyst, legendary gold. Each rarity also has a written label, engraved pattern and one/two/three/four-point seal. Boss cards add a separate crowned seal.

Type: Georgia for names/headings; Trebuchet MS / system sans for controls. These are system stacks, with no remote font requests. Functional labels use sentence case.

Desktop: shallow top rail, central arena, narrow intent column, illustrated hand below. Board and commands stay visible. Phone: compact expandable intent, horizontal hand, selected card detail without covering the needed targets.

## Design review

The new stone carving, shield/crown/mitre/horse silhouettes and bronze reliefs avoid OVERDRIVE's ceramic power collars and REMORSE marble. Bosses have distinct structures: Regent's four feet and suspended shell, Spire's blade frame, Archivist's paired book plates. Arena and cards carry the identity instead of repeated dashboard panels.

## Asset production

Concrete GLB, WAV and SVG assets are generated before product rendering. All are original procedural/vector work for this repository; no third-party character, model, recording or texture data. Mesh and material names identify armour, core and unit surfaces. Generators and manifests record reproducible source.

## Motion and access

Animations follow committed events; reduced motion preserves highlights and damage. Shield, immunity, fire, spent activation and legal targets use shapes/patterns as well as colour. Accessible square buttons serve 3D and 2D. Card rules and costs never depend on hovering.
